Undergraduate Thesis: Interpretable Biomarker Discovery for Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor (ICI) Response in Bladder Cancer

2025

Authors: Sadia Ruhama*, Syed Ayman Shahbad*, Ahnaf Warid (*equal contribution)

Supervisor: Dr. Md. Ashraful Alam

Developed an ensemble feature selection framework combining SVM-RFE, RF-RFE, Mutual Information, and LR-RFE with frequency-based majority voting to identify predictive biomarkers for pembrolizumab response in bladder cancer. Applied to the GSE111636 dataset (11 patients, 70,000+ gene features) using nested Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation to address the "curse of dimensionality" in low-sample, high-dimensional gene expression data. Benchmarked against deep learning approaches (Concrete Autoencoder, L1-regularized Neural Network), the ensemble method identified a stable 11-gene biomarker signature (including TOP2A, ZEB2, PTCH1, CDCA5), validated through DAVID functional enrichment analysis linking the genes to immune checkpoint regulation and tumor progression pathways.

Keywords: Bladder Cancer · Ensemble Feature Selection · Biomarker Discovery · Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor (ICI) Therapy · High-Dimensional Gene Expression · Interpretable Machine Learning

Integrative scRNA-seq and scATAC-seq Analysis Identifies a Robust BCG-Induced HSPC Reprogramming Signature

2026

Authors: Sadia Ruhama

Supervisor: Dr. Swakkhar Shatabda

Applied Multi-Omics Factor Analysis (MOFA+) to paired scRNA-seq and scATAC-seq data from 653 HSPCs isolated from two NMIBC patients (GSE295308), sampled before and after BCG instillation, to jointly decompose transcriptional and chromatin accessibility programmes into shared latent factors. Identified Factor 1 as the dominant BCG-associated latent factor (Pearson r = +0.495, permutation p = 0.001), with the BCG signal confirmed independent of sequencing depth confounders via multiple regression (coefficient = +0.351, p = 2.35 × 10⁻²³, R² = 0.698). Derived a versioned BCG-induced HSPC gene signature, with a 48-gene reproducible core confirmed through leave-one-out cross-patient MOFA+ modelling, and functionally annotated via NF-κB/RELB motif enrichment and pathways spanning hematopoiesis, interferon response, and transcriptional regulation. External validation across four independent cohorts (TCGA-BLCA, GSE176178, GSE199471, GSE184241) revealed a fundamental tissue-context dependency: the signature functions as a trained immunity marker in circulating HSPCs and monocytes, but correlates with worse clinical outcomes when its genes are expressed in tumour tissue, reflecting a known stemness-versus-immunity paradox.

Keywords: Single-cell Genomics · Multi-omics Integration · MOFA+ · Hematopoietic Stem and Progenitor Cells (HSPCs) · BCG Immunotherapy · Trained Immunity · Cancer Immunology · Bladder Cancer

Advancement in Detection of Melanoma: A Systematic Literature Review on CNN Utilization

2024

Authors: Sadia Ruhama, Anit Paul

Supervisor: Dr. Farida Chowdhury

Conducted a Systematic Literature Review (SLR) following PRISMA, screening 109,275 records across six digital libraries and narrowing to 18 studies to examine CNNs in melanoma detection. The review found that larger, more diverse datasets (e.g., ISIC 2019, 25,000+ images) consistently improved classification performance, and that combining data augmentation with Bayesian tuning boosted Inception-v3's F1 score from 54.10% to 96.29%. Overall, CNNs proved more consistent and scalable than traditional methods (visual inspection, dermoscopy, biopsy), which are limited to 75-84% diagnostic accuracy, though data availability, interpretability, and overfitting remain key limitations.

Keywords: Melanoma Detection · Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) · Systematic Literature Review (SLR) · Skin Cancer · Medical Image Classification · Deep Learning · Computer-Aided Diagnosis
